## Background

This is a Cafe24 app built with former colleagues. It allows e-commerce store owners to place interactive pins on product images. It passed the Cafe24 App Store review and is currently offering all features for free during its beta period.

## Problem Definition

As a developer, building features was familiar. The problem came after that. Simply building and publishing a product doesn't magically bring in users.

## Failure Conditions

- Failing to pass the App Store review process
- Users installing the app but creating zero pins
- Inability to explain what worked and what didn't using data

## Approach

I tackled non-development areas right from the start. I wrote PRDs, refined landing page copy, created Instagram card news, and ran beta outreach targeting existing Charla merchants.

## Implementation

- Integrated with Cafe24 App API and passed the App Store review
- Created landing page and marketing assets (card news)
- Designed analytics and conversion tracking infrastructure

## Results

The App Store registration and beta launch went as planned, but the actual user response was lower than expected. This experience made me realize that "building something" and "getting people to use it" are completely different problems. Currently, I am reading *Lean Analytics* and reorganizing the metrics to track at each stage.

## Lessons Learned

Knowing how to build a product and acquiring users are entirely separate skill sets. Thanks to designing the conversion tracking structure in advance, I established a starting point to investigate "why it isn't being used" through data rather than guesswork.
